# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. Bug reports, ideas, translations and code are all welcome.

- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an issue and describe your TV or TV box, its Android version, and your phone browser.
- **Want to add a language?** Follow [docs/TRANSLATING.md](docs/TRANSLATING.md).
- **Want to change code?** Read [docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md), keep the change small and focused, and run
  `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest` before opening a pull request.

A few rules of the house:

- All text the user sees goes in the language files, never directly in code.
- The app must keep working with no internet connection.
- By contributing you agree that your work is released under the project's [MIT licence](LICENSE).
