🏀 Basketball1 or 2 players

Move your ball and score in the hoop on the far side. Go in gently: a fast ball bounces off. 90 seconds.

Red scores on the right and blue on the left. A goal within 5 seconds is worth 3 points, within 10 seconds 2 points, after that 1 point. If no second player joins, the computer plays instead.

🏒 Air Hockey1 or 2 players

Move the mallet with your finger and hit the puck into the other goal. First to 7 goals wins.

Red plays on the left and black on the right. If no second player joins, the computer plays instead.

🐍 Snake1 or 2 players

Eat to grow. Do not hit yourself or the other snake. Leave one edge of the screen and you come back from the opposite one. 90 seconds, the longest snake wins.

A red pellet is 1 point and a golden one is 3. You can put your own face on the snake with the ⚙️ button inside the game.
